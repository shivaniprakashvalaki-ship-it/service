About HomeServe

Finding a trustworthy plumber, electrician or carpenter usually means asking around, making phone calls and hoping for the best. There is no easy way to check qualifications, compare prices or track a booking.

HomeServe is a single place where customers can find administrator-verified professionals in their locality, compare ratings and charges, book a time slot, track the job and leave an honest review once the work is done.

How we build trust

  • Providers submit identity and skill documents; an administrator approves them before they can be booked.
  • Only customers with a completed booking can leave a review, and only one per booking.
  • Confirmed jobs can't overlap, so providers are never double-booked.
  • Complaints go straight to the administrators.
